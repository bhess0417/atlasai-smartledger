# Sprint 37 — Atlas Brand Alignment

## Goal

Create a consistent Atlas AI visual identity across the application by replacing legacy gold and green styling with the approved Atlas brand palette while preserving clear status and financial indicators.

## Sprint 37 Scope

- Establish one consistent Atlas AI application color palette
- Replace legacy gold accents with Atlas brand colors
- Replace decorative green accents that are not meaningful status indicators
- Preserve green where it communicates positive financial performance or success
- Preserve red where it communicates risk, loss, or required attention
- Standardize buttons, badges, borders, cards, navigation, and hover states
- Improve visual consistency across the executive dashboard
- Preserve all Sprint 36 authentication and workspace functionality

## Definition of Done

Sprint 37 is complete when the Atlas AI interface uses a consistent brand palette across navigation, cards, buttons, badges, borders, and interactive states, while green and red remain reserved for meaningful financial or status information and all Sprint 36 functionality remains intact.
